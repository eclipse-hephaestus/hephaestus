## Get involved

Eclipse SDV Hephaestus welcomes contributors from automotive, tooling, research, and open source communities.

Good first contributions include documentation fixes, example workflows, integration recipes, and validation pipeline improvements.

Find new content on the [Project website](https://projects.eclipse.org/projects/automotive.hephaestus)
