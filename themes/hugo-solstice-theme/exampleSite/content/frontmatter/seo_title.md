---
title: "seo_title"
description: "The SEO-optimized title used in the `<title>` tag and social metadata. The site-level `seo_title_suffix` is appended when set."
type: "doc_param"
category: "metadata"
param_type: "string"
accepted_values:
  - Any string value
hide_sidebar: true
---

When `seo_title` is not set, the page's `headline` or `title` is used instead.

## Example

```yaml
seo_title: "Getting Started with Eclipse IoT"
```
